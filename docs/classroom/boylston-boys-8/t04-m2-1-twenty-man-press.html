Topic 2.1

Twenty (Man Press)

Our full-court man press. Pick up, deny, pressure, protect the rim.

Coach [Name]·7 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.2Man-to-Man Habits

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Know all five jobs, including the safety.
  • 02Know the one place we double-team.

Play cards

Twenty

Call: Twenty
  1. 01Pick up your man right after we score. Sprint, find him, get between him and the ball.
  2. 02Deny the inbound pass with your arm in the passing lane. Hands high and active. Deflections win.
  3. 03Pressure the ball. Get up on the dribbler and make him speed-dribble. Don't back off.
  4. 04Only double-team in a trap spot (corner or half-court sideline) or when the dribbler loses control. When the ball leaves the trap, turn and sprint.
  5. 05One player (X5) always protects the basket. No layups, ever. Past half court, it's normal man-to-man.
CounterSignal: two fists up. Circles are them, X is us.

Coaching points

    Coach
