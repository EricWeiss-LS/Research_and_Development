Topic 2.3

Rock and Hand

Two ways to get the ball moving when the defense sags or denies the wing.

Coach [Name]·8 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.3Backdoor2.1Drive and Kick

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Set a legal ball screen: feet wide, arms in, stop before contact.
  • 02Read in front or behind on a hand-off.

Play cards

Rock (ball screen)

Call: Rock
  1. 012 runs from the wing and sets a screen on 1's defender. Feet wide, arms in, don't move.
  2. 021 waits for the screen, then dribbles hard off 2's shoulder (no gap!).
  3. 032 rolls to the basket with his hand up for a pass.
  4. 043 slides over to fill the top so 1 has a safe pass back out.
  5. 051 reads it: layup, pass to 2 rolling, or kick to the corner if that defender helps. Then back to Cutters.
CounterSignal: hold up a fist. Screener: stop before contact or it's a moving screen. Ball handler: wait for it.

Hand (dribble hand-off)

Call: Hand
  1. 011 dribbles at the wing (2). This is the same start as Backdoor.
  2. 02Read the defender: if 2's defender is denying (in front), 2 goes Back. If he's playing behind or sagging, 2 comes up to meet the ball.
  3. 031 hands the ball to 2 (stop, plant, hand it off like a pitch). Then 1 replaces on the wing.
  4. 042 takes the hand-off at full speed and attacks the middle: layup, or kick to the corner if help comes.
  5. 053 fills the top. Then back to Cutters.
CounterSignal: open hand, all 5 fingers, overhead. Dribble at you = read your defender. In front of you: back cut. Behind you: come get the hand-off.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach

    Both plays start with the same picture: the ball coming at the wing. The wing's defender decides which one it is.
