Pick-and-pop for our shooter. Our best shot against tough man-to-man.

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Know the setup: who's at the top, the wing, and the corners.
  • 02Read the screen four ways: under, over, switch, double.

Play cards

Superman

Call: Superman
  1. 01Set up: our best ball handler and driver at the top (1). Our best outside shooter on the wing (2). Our best rebounders in the corners (4 and 5).
  2. 022 sets a ball screen on 1's defender, just like Rock. But instead of rolling to the rim, 2 pops back out behind the 3-point line at the top.
  3. 031 reads it: defender goes under the screen = shoot. Defender chases over = drive. They switch = drive on the slower man.
  4. 04They double-team 1? 2 is wide open popping out. Pass to 2 for the shot.
  5. 05Corners: if your defender helps on the drive, cut along the baseline for a dump-off layup. On any shot, both corners crash the boards for the put-back.
CounterSignal: hold up 3 fingers. They double 1: pass to 2 popping out.

Coaching points

    Coach
