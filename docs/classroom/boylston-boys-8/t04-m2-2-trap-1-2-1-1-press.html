Topic 2.2

Trap (1-2-1-1 Press)

A zone press for a spark. Full court only. Match at half court.

Coach [Name]·7 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1League Defense Rules2.1Twenty (Man Press)

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Know the five spots in the 1-2-1-1.
  • 02Know what "Match!" means and when to yell it.

Play cards

Trap

Call: Trap
  1. 01Set up: X1 on the inbounder, X2 and X3 at the free-throw line extended, X4 near half court, X5 deep.
  2. 02Let the pass go to the side. Then X1 and the closest wing trap him near the sideline. Arms out, use your feet, don't foul.
  3. 03X4 covers the pass up the sideline. The other wing covers the middle. X5 stays back.
  4. 04Ball gets past us or over half court? Yell "Match!" and everyone finds a man. No zone in the half court!
CounterSignal: cross your arms in an X over your head. Never let them pass to the middle.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach

    This press gives up a layup now and then to get steals. We run it when we need a spark late, not all game.
