Pass, Cut, Replace

How every Cutters possession starts, against any man-to-man defense.

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Walk through pass, cut, and replace from every spot.
  • 02Know who fills what when the top cuts.

Play cards

Pass, cut, replace

Call: Cutters
  1. 011 passes to the wing (2).
  2. 021 basket cuts straight to the rim, hand up for a return pass.
  3. 03No pass? 1 keeps going and fills the spot in the opposite corner.
  4. 043 replaces 1 at the top. 5 replaces 3 on the wing. Wait until 1 is almost at the rim before you move.
  5. 05Now 2 has the ball on the wing and the whole thing starts again.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach

    Every cut finishes at the rim. Fill the spot, don't drift into it.
