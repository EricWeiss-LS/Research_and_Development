Topic 2.1

Delay

We're ahead in the last 2 minutes. Cutters, spread wide, layups only.

Play cards

Delay

Call: Delay
  1. 01Run Cutters, but spread out wider than normal (deep corners, wide wings).
  2. 02Keep passing and cutting. Every pass makes the defense chase.
  3. 03Only shoot layups. No jump shots unless Coach says so.
  4. 04Protect the ball. Two hands on every catch. If they trap, pass out of it right away.
  5. 05If they foul, step to the line and make them. Our best free-throw shooters should be on the floor.
CounterSignal: arms out wide. A turnover is worse than a held ball. Patience wins this.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach

    Cutters is our delay offense. We don't need a new play, we need patience.
