Drag, Away, and Post

Three plays we add once the core is solid. Each one solves a specific problem.

Later plays

Situations

Live
Situation
Point guard picked up hard at half court
His defender is all over himWings are covered10 seconds to cross
Pause. What do we call?
?
Drag. The open-side wing sprints up and screens at half court so 1 can get into the front court downhill. Use the screen early, not at 9 seconds.

Drag

Call: Drag
  1. 011 keeps his dribble alive near half court. Don't pick it up! Turn your body to protect the ball.
  2. 02The wing on the open side (2) sprints up and sets a ball screen on 1's defender, right at half court.
  3. 031 dribbles hard off the screen into the front court, attacking downhill.
  4. 042 pops back out toward the top. If they double-team 1 off the screen, 2 is wide open: pass to him and he attacks.
  5. 05Once we're in the front court, we're in 5-Out. Run Cutters.
CounterSignal: drag your hand across your chest.
Situation
Defense is sagging and sitting on our cuts
Cuts aren't getting anyone openWe need an open jump shot
Pause. What do we call?
?
Away. Instead of cutting after the pass, the passer screens away. The screened player comes up to the top for the shot, or back cuts if his defender jumps out.

Away

Call: Away
  1. 011 passes to the left wing (3).
  2. 02Instead of cutting, 1 runs away from the ball and sets a screen on 2's defender.
  3. 032 sets up his man (step toward the basket first), then comes off the screen up to the top.
  4. 043 passes to 2 for the shot. If 2's defender jumps out to deny him, 2 back cuts to the rim instead.
  5. 051 pops back out to the wing so we're in 5-Out again.
CounterSignal: wave your hand away. If your defender denies you on the screen, don't fight it. Back cut.
Situation
Our bigger, stronger player has a small defender
Mismatch on the block3 seconds in the paint is a violation
Pause. What do we call?
?
Post. He gets permission to post up for 2 seconds after his cut. Count it out loud. If nothing comes, clear to the corner like normal.

Post

Call: Post
  1. 011 passes to the wing (2) and basket cuts, like normal Cutters.
  2. 02Instead of running through, 1 stops on the ball-side block and posts up: wide base, backside into the defender, hand up.
  3. 032 looks inside. Pass to 1 for a power move.
  4. 04No pass after 2 seconds? 1 clears to the far corner like normal.
  5. 053 waits to replace at the top until 1 starts to clear. Leaving early messes up the timing.
CounterSignal: tap your shoulder. Count it: one-thousand, two-thousand, GO.
