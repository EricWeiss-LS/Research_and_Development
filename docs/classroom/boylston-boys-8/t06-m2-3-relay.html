Topic 2.3

Relay

Full court with 8 to 12 seconds left. They just scored or the ball is under their basket.

Play cards

Relay

Call: Relay
  1. 013 inbounds (he can run the baseline after a made basket).
  2. 021 fakes toward the ball, then cuts up and screens for 2. 2 fakes deep, then curls off the screen to the ball. 1 rolls back to the ball after screening.
  3. 033 passes to 2 first, then 1 if 2 is covered.
  4. 04At half court, 5 screens for 4. The guard who catches the inbound passes right away to 4.
  5. 051 and 2 sprint wide up the sidelines. 5 rolls to the rim. 4 hits 5, hits a guard, or drives it himself. 3 trails for the rebound.
CounterSignal: point down the court. Under 3 seconds? Same setup, but 3 throws it long to 4 or 5 and they shoot right away.

    Coach
