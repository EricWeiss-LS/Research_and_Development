Topic 1.3

Backdoor

When your defender denies you, don't fight it. Cut behind it.

Coach [Name]·6 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.2Pass, Cut, Replace

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Recognize the two backdoor cues.
  • 02Make the cut and know the pass that comes with it.

Play cards

Backdoor

Call: Back
  1. 01Your defender steps up to the 3-point line to stop the pass. That's your cue.
  2. 02Or the ball handler dribbles right at you. That's also your cue.
  3. 03Take one step up to sell it, plant your outside foot, and cut hard behind your defender to the rim.
  4. 04The passer throws a bounce pass to you for the layup.
  5. 05No pass? Keep going and fill the open baseline spot. Everyone replaces like normal.

    Coach
