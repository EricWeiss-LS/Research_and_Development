Topic 2.1

Drive and Kick

Cutters opens the lane on purpose. Use it.

Coach [Name]·7 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1The 5 Rules of Cutters1.2Pass, Cut, Replace

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Drive the gap in two hard dribbles.
  • 02Know where the wings and corners go when someone drives.

Play cards

Drive and kick

Call: Go
  1. 011 attacks the gap and drives toward the rim.
  2. 02The two wings drift up toward the top so they're open for a pass back out.
  3. 03The corners stay in the corners. Don't move toward the driver!
  4. 04If a defender helps on the drive, kick the ball to the player that defender left (usually the corner).
  5. 05No help? Finish the layup. If the kick goes to the corner, he dribbles up toward the wing to fix the spacing.
CounterSignal: point at the basket.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach

    Drive to score, not to dribble. Two hard dribbles and get to the rim.
