Topic 2.1

2-Up

Against any full-court press, man or zone. Same jobs every time.

Play cards

2-Up

Call: 2-Up
  1. 011 and 2 (guards) start near the free-throw line and screen for each other to get open for the inbound. Seal your man if they switch.
  2. 023 inbounds to the guard who's open, on the side, not in the corner.
  3. 03On the catch, 4 (ball-side forward) flashes up toward the ball. 5 (far-side forward) runs deep to keep the defense honest.
  4. 041 passes to 4. The guard who didn't catch it (2) sprints down the middle. 4 hits 2 and we attack.
  5. 05If 4 isn't open: reverse the ball back to 3. That's the cue for 5 to flash up on the other side. 3 hits 5, 2 cuts the middle, 5 hits 2.
CounterSignal: two fingers pointing up. Trapped? Pivot, ball high, look for the safety (3) behind you. Never pick up your dribble in a corner.
