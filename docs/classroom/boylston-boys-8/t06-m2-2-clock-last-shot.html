Topic 2.2

Clock (Last Shot)

Tied or down 1 to 3, 30 seconds or less. We want the last good shot.

Coach [Name]·7 min·Not viewed
Builds onT02 2.2Superman2.1Delay

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Know the three numbers: hold to 10, screen at 8, shot up by 3.
  • 02Know the first look when we're down 3.

Play cards

Clock

Call: Clock
  1. 01Run Delay (Cutters, layups only) until about 10 seconds left. A layup earlier is fine. Take it.
  2. 02At 8 seconds, run Superman: 2 sets the ball screen for 1 and pops out.
  3. 031 reads it like Superman: drive for the layup, pull up and shoot, or hit 2 popping out.
  4. 04The shot must be up by 3 seconds so we get a rebound chance.
  5. 05When the shot goes up, 4 and 5 crash the boards. Tip it back in.
CounterSignal: tap your wrist. Down by 3? 2 popping out is the first look, because it's a 3. 1 only drives if 2 is covered.
