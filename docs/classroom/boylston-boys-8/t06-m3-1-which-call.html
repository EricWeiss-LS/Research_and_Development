Coach's questions

  1. Coach · 01
    Down 2, 25 seconds left, we have the ball. What's the call, and what does 1 do at 8 seconds?
    What I'm listening forClock. At 8, 2 sets the ball screen and pops. 1 reads it: drive, pull up, or hit 2. Shot up by 3.
  2. Coach · 02
    Down 3, 25 seconds left. Same call. What changes?
    What I'm listening forThe first look is 2 popping out, because it's a 3. 1 only drives if 2 is covered.
  3. Coach · 03
    Up 11, 1:30 left, they just scored. Press or not?
    What I'm listening forNo. Man up is still on at 10 or more. Run Delay when we get it.
