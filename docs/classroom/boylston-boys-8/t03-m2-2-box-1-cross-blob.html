Topic 2.2

Box 1 = Cross (BLOB)

Baseline inbound under our basket. Easy to learn and gets a layup a lot.

Coach [Name]·7 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1Inbound Rules

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Set up the box without being told.
  • 02Screen a person, not air.

Play cards

Box 1 (Cross)

Call: Box 1
  1. 01Set up a box: 5 and 4 on the blocks, 3 and 1 at the elbows. 2 inbounds on the right.
  2. 02On the slap, 5 sets a cross screen for 4. 4 cuts to the ball side of the rim.
  3. 03At the same time, 3 sets a cross screen for 1. 1 cuts to the ball-side elbow.
  4. 04After screening, 5 and 3 turn to the ball and step into an open spot. The screener is often the one who's open!
  5. 052 hits whoever is open. Then fill to 5-Out and run Cutters.
CounterSignal: inbounder holds up 1 finger.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach

    Before the slap, screeners look to see who's guarding the man you'll screen. Screen a person, not air.
