Stack (SLOB)

Objective

Play cards

Stack

Call: Stack
  1. 01Four players line up in a stack across from the inbounder (4). Order: 1, 2, 3, 5.
  2. 02On the slap: 1 cuts hard toward our basket to catch. 2 cuts up the stack to the ball (decoy). 3 breaks away to the far wing. 5 floats back as the safety.
  3. 034 passes to 1. If 1 is covered, throw to 5 (safety).
  4. 041 dribbles toward the middle and the other side. 5 cuts hard to the far block and posts up. 4 steps in and screens for 3.
  5. 051 looks for 5 posting first, then 3 coming off the screen. Nothing there? Pull it out and run Cutters.
CounterSignal: inbounder holds up 4 fingers. Inbounder: fake a pass first.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach

    It gets the ball in safely against pressure and gives us a quick scoring chance. Both matter.
