Topic 2.2

Middle

When they wait at half court and trap the ball handler as he crosses.

Coach [Name]·7 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1Press Break Rules

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Bring the ball up with two guards 15 feet apart.
  • 02Know the two outs when the trap comes.

Play cards

Middle

Call: Middle
  1. 01Bring the ball up with two guards side by side (1 and 2), about 15 feet apart. 3 starts at the free-throw line. 4 and 5 are in the corners.
  2. 02If they trap 1: pivot, ball up high, protect it. Don't try to dribble out.
  3. 033 flashes to the middle, just above the top of the key, with his hands up. Pass to 3. Or pass across to 2.
  4. 043 catches, turns, and faces the basket. The trap left someone open: hit 4 or 5 in the corner, or drive it himself.
  5. 05Once we're past the trap, attack the rim before they recover. Then run Cutters.
CounterSignal: tap the top of your head. Over half court we can't pass back behind the line (over-and-back). The middle and the other guard are your outs.

Coaching points

    Coach
