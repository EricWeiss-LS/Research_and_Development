Topic 3.2

Defending BLOBs

Make them throw it to the top. Defender near the rim, switch everything, or flash a 2-3 zone.

Coach Kevin Keto·8 min·Not viewed
Builds on2.1Post Denial: 3/4 Front and 1/2 Front

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Know our goal on a BLOB: make them throw it to the top and run their main offense.
  • 02Take away the under the rim pass as the inbounds passer's defender, and switch everything.
  • 03Read when we play man and when we play a 2-3 zone, then switch back to man on the catch.

BLOB Words

Key terms

BLOB

Also called Baseline in-bounds

A baseline inbounds play. Teams run well-thought-out plays here and are in perfect position to score.

In-bounds defender near the rim

Also called Inbounds passer's defender

You guard the inbounds passer from along the lane line where it meets the baseline, to take away the under the rim pass.

Man or Zone on a BLOB

Reads

Situation
We stick to man-to-man on the inbounds.
Read
The danger is the easy pass and score under the rim.
Action
The inbounds passer's defender sits along the lane line at the baseline. Force the pass out to the wing.
Situation
They run up-screens and cross-screens.
Read
Those screens are hard to defend on BLOB plays.
Action
Switch everything. Stay below your player and on the basket side.
Situation
We call a 2-3 zone on the inbounds.
Read
The zone floods the key and takes away easy baskets under the rim.
Action
Play the 2-3 until the ball is inbounded to the wing or the top, then switch straight back into man-to-man.

Keys

  1. 1
    Stay below and on the basket side.
    When we switch everything, that is how we stop the easy score.
  2. 2
    Back to man on the catch.
    The 2-3 is only for the inbounds. Once the ball is at the wing or top, we are in man-to-man.
