Topic 2.1

Post Denial: 3/4 Front and 1/2 Front

Above the free-throw line we 3/4 front. Below it we 1/2 front from the baseline side.

Coach Kevin Keto·7 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1Keeping the Ball Out of the Post

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Play 3/4 front on the line between the ball and your opponent when the ball is above the free-throw line.
  • 02Play 1/2 front from the baseline side when the ball is below the free-throw line.
  • 03Do your job from low-I to pick off passes over the top.

Post Denial Positions

Key terms

3/4 front

Also called Ball above the free-throw line

You sit three quarters in front of the post player, on the line between the ball and your opponent. We use it when the ball is above the free-throw line.

Tap to see it
3/4 front

The entry pass has to be very accurate, and you still have time to get between them and the basket.

Tap to flip back

1/2 front

Also called Ball below the free-throw line, baseline side

You half front the post player from the baseline side. We use it when the ball is below the free-throw line.

Tap to see it
1/2 front

The pass is shorter now, so only half fronting lets you still get position behind if you can't deflect the pass.

Tap to flip back

Low-I

Also called Help position

The help defender down low on the weak side. When the post is 3/4 fronted, this player picks off any passes made over the top.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach Kevin Keto

    We do not front the low post. We 3/4 front when the ball is above the free-throw line and 1/2 front from the baseline side when it is below.
