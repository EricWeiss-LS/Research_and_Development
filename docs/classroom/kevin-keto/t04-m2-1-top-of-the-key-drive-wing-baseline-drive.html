Topic 2.1

Top of the Key Drive & Wing Baseline Drive

Low-I meets the driver outside the paint, High-I rotates down, the top defender drops to intercept.

Coach Kevin Keto·8 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1Help Always Comes from the Weak Side

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Do your job on a top of the key drive: lowest weak-side defender steps across, one pass away stunts, x1 rotates down.
  • 02Do your job on a wing baseline drive: Low-I meets the driver outside the paint, High-I rotates down, the top defender drops to intercept.
  • 03Keep ball-side corner defenders home on their man.

Drive Rotations

Play cards

Top of the Key Drive

Call: Lowest weak-side steps across
  1. 01The drive from the top of the key through the foul line is the hardest penetration to defend. We never want to let it happen.
  2. 02x1 at the top is beat and the dribbler gets into the lane.
  3. 03x5 is the lowest on the weak side, so x5 steps across to defend the basketball.
  4. 04x3 is one pass away and does not help. x3 can quickly stunt to make the driver hesitate or pick up the ball, but must not leave 3.
  5. 05Help always comes from the weak side, so x4 steps out and takes away the corner three-point attempt.
  6. 06When x5 steps up to help, x1 immediately rotates down and is now the player who closes out if the pass goes across court to 3 or 5.
CounterThis drive happens against a great point guard, when the on-ball defender plays too close to his man, or when we allow ball reversals and our rotations and closeouts are poor. Take those away and it doesn't happen.

Wing Baseline Drive

Call: Low-I meets, High-I rotates down
  1. 01Ball on the wing: ball side and weak side are clearly set, which is great for the defense.
  2. 02x1 at the top of the key denies the ball reversal.
  3. 03The ball is at the free-throw line or lower, so High-I and Low-I are set in the key.
  4. 04x4 in the corner with 4 does not help off a ball-side corner player.
  5. 05Low-I (x5) helps on the drive and meets the dribbler just outside the paint. If he picks up the dribble, x5 and x2 trap him.
  6. 06High-I (x3) rotates down and prevents the pass to 5 in the corner.
  7. 07x1 drops down to a spot where he can intercept a lob pass to 3 or 1, or defend either of them if the skip pass is made over the top.
