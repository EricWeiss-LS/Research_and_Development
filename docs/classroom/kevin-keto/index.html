Classroom

What we study

Six tracks, each taught in three stages. Work through a track in order: learn the language and the rules first, then how we run it, then when to use it in a game.

FoundationWhat is it? Terms, rules, and signals.
CoreHow do we run it? Your job in each play.
AppliedWhen, and why? Reads and decisions.

Track 01Motion Offense: Entry & Initial Action

M1 Foundation What we want and where we go

1.1Motion Offense GoalsLayup or open three, move the ball, read and cut hard, always have an outlet.1.2Transitioning into Spots1 catches at FT line extended, 2 and 3 race to the corners, 4 inbounds and trails, 5 races to the rim.

M2 Core How we run it

2.1Initiating Offense: 1 & 4 Pick and PopThe offense starts when 4 catches the ball. Screen and rescreen, 5 slides opposite, 2 and 3 spot up.2.2Violent Cuts: Butt Screen, Sprint Screen4 hits the sprinting wing, 3 SPRINTS around the butt screen, 4 SPRINTS into his screen.

M3 Applied Reads under pressure

3.1Scoring ReadsRim-cutting wing, popping 1, or short roll face up. 1 pops behind the screen and never sets his feet.3.2Pick, Roll, Replace1 must get middle, 3 replaces early, 4 rolls correctly. 1 can play with his food before he eats.

Track 02Motion Offense: When the Initial Action Breaks Down

M1 Foundation Play basketball

1.1Breakdown RulesDon't try to regain the pattern. Pass and screen away, don't over dribble, don't pass and stand.

M2 Core Our go-to actions

2.1Staggered Away ScreensStagger whenever possible. On post hits, the passer and the next closest teammate stagger away, Davidson Wildcats style.2.2Back Screen Our 4 into the PostLook for chances to back screen the 4 into the post.

M3 Applied Pressure and shots

3.1Pressure, DHOs and the GlassWhen you're pressured, seek a DHO. On shots, 3, 4 and 5 crash while 1 and 2 get back.

Track 03No Middle Man-to-Man: Rules & Positioning

M1 Foundation The rules and the language

1.1The 5 Man-to-Man RulesNo middle penetration, no ball reversals, no help one pass away, no face cuts, move when the ball moves.1.2Defense DictionaryOn-ball, deny, help, ball-you-man, split line, High-I, Low-I, stunt, jump to the ball, banana closeout.

M2 Core Our jobs on every possession

2.1On-Ball, Deny and Help DefenseContain and overplay the middle, one hand and foot in the lane when one pass away, ball-you-man when two away.2.2Positions by Ball Location: Top, Wing, CornerGet it out of the top, never let it reverse, and keep two help defenders on the split line.

M3 Applied Coach's choices

3.1Top to Wing Pass: Allow or Deny?We allow the pass to commit the ball to one side. Know when denying it disrupts their offense.

Track 04No Middle Man-to-Man: Dribble Penetration & Rotations

M1 Foundation Where help comes from

1.1Help Always Comes from the Weak SideHigh-I and Low-I on the split line. The lowest weak-side defender steps across, and one pass away only stunts.

M2 Core Rotations we drill every practice

2.1Top of the Key Drive & Wing Baseline DriveLow-I meets the driver outside the paint, High-I rotates down, the top defender drops to intercept.2.2Corner Baseline DriveDeny the reverse pass, Low-I traps with x4, x3 drops, x1 splits two and closes on the skip.

M3 Applied When it gets messy

3.1Wing Middle DriveSlide vertically and cut it off or force it to the top. If it's inevitable, the lowest weak-side player steps across.3.2Drives with a Low or High Post DefenderThe post defender traps from the 1/2 front, or denies the high post and doesn't help. Rotations shift behind them.3.3Skip Pass RotationsBanana-cut closeouts so there's no middle. Everyone sprints on the flight of the ball.

Track 05No Middle: Spartan Defensive System

M1 Foundation The seven pillars

1.1No Middle: Control the Middle-ThirdFull court pressure, push dribblers to the sidelines, and the opposing PG can't dribble through the jump circle.

M2 Core How Spartans defend

2.1Elite Closeouts & Deny Lane PenetrationHigh active hands, choppy feet, attack the high hip. Get in his pocket, voice pressure, force to short corners.2.2Pack GapsJump to ball, stunt and recover, 2 passes away = midline, melt to the ball on baseline penetration.

M3 Applied Win the possession

3.1Ice Ball ScreensThe on-ball defender makes the dribbler reject and go baseline, bigs drop, and we trap 1-2 steps outside the lane.3.2Rebound! & Get TurkeysUltimate physicality, two hands, 10 Spartan hands on every rebound. 3 consecutive stops = turkey, 7 per game.

Track 06Man-to-Man: Post, Transition, Inbounds & Pick and Roll

M1 Foundation Don't let it get there

1.1Keeping the Ball Out of the PostBeat them down the floor, great on-ball defense, push them off the block.

M2 Core Our positioning

2.1Post Denial: 3/4 Front and 1/2 FrontAbove the free-throw line we 3/4 front. Below it we 1/2 front from the baseline side.2.2Transition Defense: Three Crash, Two BackNever stand in no man's land. Two get back as a top defender and a bottom defender.

M3 Applied Special situations

3.1Trapping a Dominant Low Post PlayerLow-I traps high side, x2 drops, x1 becomes the interceptor, ball-side stays in complete denial.3.2Defending BLOBsMake them throw it to the top. Defender near the rim, switch everything, or flash a 2-3 zone.3.3Defending the Pick and RollHedge and fight over, switch, or go under: know the choice and the cost.
