Topic 1.1

Help Always Comes from the Weak Side

High-I and Low-I on the split line. The lowest weak-side defender steps across, and one pass away only stunts.

Coach Kevin Keto·6 min·Not viewed

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Name and recognize High-I, Low-I, the split line, and the ball-you-man stance.
  • 02Recognize who is one pass away and who is two passes away when the ball is on a wing or in a corner.
  • 03Identify the lowest weak-side defender who steps across on a drive.

Help Defense Words

Key terms

Help Defense

Also called Two or more passes away

Any defender two or more passes away from the basketball is playing help defense. Your spot changes with where the ball is and where your man is.

Ball-You-Man

Also called One hand on your man, one hand on the ball

The stance every help defender plays in. Point one hand at your opponent and one hand at the basketball, and see both with your peripheral vision at all times.

Split Line

Also called The line down the middle of the court

When the ball is on either wing or in a corner, there should always be 2 help defenders with at least one foot on the split line.

Low-I

Also called Help defender close to the rim in the paint

The help defender on the split line close to the rim. When the ball is on the weak side and below the free-throw line, one player two passes away is here.

Stunt

Also called Fake help, then get back

What the defender one pass away does on a drive. Quickly fake at the driver to make them hesitate or pick up the ball, but never leave your direct opponent.

How We Help

Principles

  1. 01
    Help always comes from the weak side.
    Looks likeOn a drive, the weak-side defenders rotate. Ball-side players stay with their man.
  2. 02
    Two feet of help on the split line.
    Looks likeBall on a wing or in a corner: one defender in Low-I near the rim and one in High-I near the free-throw line, each with at least one foot on the split line.
  3. 03
    Play ball-you-man when you are two passes away.
    Looks likeOne hand pointing at your man, one at the ball, both in your peripheral vision at all times.
  4. 04
    The lowest weak-side defender steps across.
    Looks likeWhen the dribbler beats his man and gets into the lane, whoever is lowest on the weak side steps across to defend the ball.
  5. 05
    One pass away only stunts.
    Looks likeYou fake at the driver to make him hesitate or pick it up, then you are right back on your man. You never leave him.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach Kevin Keto

    Remember: Help always comes from the weak side.

  2. 02
    Coach Kevin Keto

    If you can't make the correct rotations when a player drives, we will have a very ineffective man-to-man defense. We drill these every practice.
