Topic 2.2

Transition Defense: Three Crash, Two Back

Never stand in no man's land. Two get back as a top defender and a bottom defender.

Coach Kevin Keto·6 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1Keeping the Ball Out of the Post

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Know your job the moment a shot goes up: crash the glass or get back.
  • 02Get back as the top defender or the bottom defender and do that job.
  • 03Never stand and watch in no man's land.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach Kevin Keto

    You can never be a great man-to-man defensive team if you are not a great transition defense team. Get back quickly after every offensive possession.

  2. 02
    Coach Kevin Keto

    The worst thing you can do after our shot is stand and watch. Not getting back, not attacking the glass. That is no man's land, and you never want to be there.

Transition Words

Key terms

Three crash, two back

Also called Our transition rule

On every shot, three players go to the offensive glass and two players get back on defense immediately.

No man's land

Also called Standing and watching

When a player neither gets back on defense nor attacks the offensive glass after a shot. Never be here.

Keys

  1. 1
    Know before the shot whether you crash or get back.
    The secret to great transition defense is every player knowing how many go to the boards and how many retreat.
  2. 2
    Top defender slows the ball.
    Slowing the ball buys time for the three who crashed to get back.
