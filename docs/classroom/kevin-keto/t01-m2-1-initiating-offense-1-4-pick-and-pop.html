Initiating Offense: 1 & 4 Pick and Pop

The offense starts when 4 catches the ball. Screen and rescreen, 5 slides opposite, 2 and 3 spot up.

Coach Kevin Keto·6 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1Motion Offense Goals

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Run the 1 & 4 Pick and Pop in either direction.
  • 02Do your job: 5 slides opposite of where 1 is headed, 2 and 3 spot up and anticipate.
  • 03Know that the offense starts when 4 catches the ball.
