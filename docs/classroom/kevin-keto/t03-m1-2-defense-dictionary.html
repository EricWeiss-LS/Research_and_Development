Our Defensive Words

Key terms

On-ball defense

Also called Guarding the ball

You are guarding the player with the ball. Your job is to contain him and send him to the sideline or baseline.

Deny

Also called Denial, one pass away

You are one pass away and you take away the pass to your man. One hand and one foot in the passing lane, chest to your man, eyes over your lead shoulder.

Help defense

Also called Two or more passes away

You are two or more passes away from the ball. You are in ball-you-man and ready to help on a drive.

Ball-you-man

Also called Help stance

One hand pointing at your man and one hand pointing at the ball. You see both with your peripheral vision at all times.

Ball-you-man

We use it when two passes away. It is the wrong stance one pass away, because a backdoor cut would make you turn a full 180 degrees.

Split line

Also called The line down the middle of the court

The imaginary line down the middle of the floor. When the ball is on a wing or in a corner, we always have 2 help defenders with at least one foot on it.

High-I

Also called Help near the free-throw line

The help defender on the split line near the free-throw line. When the ball is in the corner, High-I gets ready to drop down and rotate if Low-I has to help.

Low-I

Also called Help close to the rim

The help defender on the split line close to the rim in the paint. Low-I is in help position, anticipating the drive.

Stunt

Also called Fake help

A quick move toward a driver by the defender one pass away to make him hesitate or pick up the dribble. You stunt, then get back. You never fully help and never leave your man.

Jump to the ball

Also called Jump to the basketball

The moment your man passes, you move toward the ball so he has to cut behind you, not across your face.

Banana closeout

Also called Banana cut closeout

On a skip pass, you close out on a curved path so the receiver cannot drive middle.
