Topic 2.2

Corner Baseline Drive

Deny the reverse pass, Low-I traps with x4, x3 drops, x1 splits two and closes on the skip.

Coach Kevin Keto·6 min·Not viewed
Builds on1.1Help Always Comes from the Weak Side

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Do your job on a corner baseline drive: deny the reverse pass, Low-I traps with x4, x3 drops.
  • 02As x1, split the two offensive players and close out on the skip.

Corner Baseline Drive

Play cards

Corner Baseline Drive

Call: Low-I traps with x4
  1. 01The rotations on baseline penetration from the corner are very similar to wing penetration.
  2. 02x2, guarding the wing player, denies the easy reverse pass back outside.
  3. 03If 4 drives baseline to the basket, x5 in Low-I steps across and sets a trap with x4.
  4. 04When that happens, x3 drops down and cuts off the passing lane to the player x5 was guarding.
  5. 05x1 drops and positions himself in between two offensive players, 1 and 3.
  6. 06If the interception is there, go for it. If not and the skip pass is made, x1 closes out on either 1 or 3.

Keys

  1. 1
    Deny the reverse pass.
    If 4 has an easy pass back outside, the trap is worthless.
  2. 2
    x3 fills x5's man.
    When Low-I leaves to trap, the player he was guarding is open under the rim unless x3 drops.
  3. 3
    x1 guards two.
    One defender in between 1 and 3 can steal the pass or close out on whoever catches the skip.
