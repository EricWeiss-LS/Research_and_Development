Topic 3.1

Wing Middle Drive

Slide vertically and cut it off or force it to the top. If it's inevitable, the lowest weak-side player steps across.

Coach Kevin Keto·8 min·Not viewed
Builds on2.1Top of the Key Drive & Wing Baseline Drive

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Make the read on a wing middle drive: cut it off vertically, force it to the top, or step across if it's inevitable.
  • 02Read when the dribbler crosses the split line and know the help defender has changed.
  • 03Stunt from one pass away without fully committing to a trap.

Reading the Middle Drive

Reads

Situation
You are on the ball on the high side and the wing drives middle.
Read
You are in position. You can beat him there.
Action
Take a large slide or two vertically and cut off the drive or draw a charge.
Situation
You are on the ball and got caught out of position.
Read
You can't cut it off, but you can still steer it.
Action
Force him to dribble to the top of the key.
Situation
The on-ball defender is really caught out of position and the drive is inevitable.
Read
The ball is coming into the middle.
Action
The lowest weak-side player steps across and challenges the shot, and the correct rotations must take place.
Situation
You are x3, one pass away, and the drive comes middle.
Read
You can bother him, but your man is a wing shooter.
Action
Stunt quickly with your back to the basketball to make him pick up the ball, only if you can close out on your man without giving up the shot. Never fully commit to a trap.
Situation
The dribbler crosses over the split line with his dribble.
Read
Ball side and weak side just flipped.
Action
The help defender changes. Help now comes from the new weak side.

Two Ways It Goes

Situations

Live
Situation
Middle drive that stays on the ball side of the split line
Ball starts on the wing with 2x5 is the lowest weak-side defenderx3 is High-I
Pause. What do we call?
?
Lowest weak-side steps across. Help always comes from the weak side. The drive has not crossed the split line, so the weak side is still the same.

Wing Middle Drive

Call: Lowest weak-side steps across
  1. 012 drives middle from the wing past x2.
  2. 02x5, the lowest weak-side player, steps across and challenges.
  3. 03The rest of the defense makes the correct rotations behind him.
Situation
Middle drive that crosses the split line
Dribbler crosses over the split lineBall side and weak side changeThe help defender changes
Pause. What do we call?
?
New weak side helps. Once the ball crosses the split line, the old ball side becomes the weak side, so the help has to come from there.

Wing Middle Drive Across the Split Line

Call: New weak side helps
  1. 012 drives middle and crosses the split line.
  2. 02The help now comes from the other side of the floor, the new weak side.
  3. 03Everyone else rotates off that help.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach Kevin Keto

    Allowing the middle drive ends up very problematic and confusing for the defense. So make sure it never happens!
