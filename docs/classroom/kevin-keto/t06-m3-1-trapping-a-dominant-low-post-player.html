Topic 3.1

Trapping a Dominant Low Post Player

Low-I traps high side, x2 drops, x1 becomes the interceptor, ball-side stays in complete denial.

Coach Kevin Keto·8 min·Not viewed
Builds on2.1Post Denial: 3/4 Front and 1/2 Front

Objective

By the end of this topic you can:

  • 01Run the low-I trap on a dominant post player and know your job in it.
  • 02Read the outlet as the interceptor: steal or deflect, or be first to close out.
  • 03Stay in complete denial on the ball side while the ball is in the post.

Coaching points

  1. 01
    Coach Kevin Keto

    Some post players are so dominant we have no choice but to send help and trap them in the post. When we do, the trap comes from the player in the low-I help position.

Trap Words

Key terms

Low-I trap

Also called Help comes from low-I

When their post catches it, our low-I helper sprints across and traps on the high side with the post defender.

Interceptor

Also called x1 in the trap

The defender who drops to pick off passes out of the trap. If there is no steal or deflection, they are first to close out on the outlet.

The Trap

Play cards

Trapping a Dominant Low Post Player

Call: Low-I trap
  1. 014 receives the pass in the post. x4 immediately establishes position behind.
  2. 02x5 sprints across and sets a trap on the high side with x4.
  3. 03x2 drops to defend 5.
  4. 04x1 drops and becomes the interceptor. Pick off any pass to 1 or 2. If none are there for a steal or deflection, be the first to close out to whoever catches the outlet.
  5. 05Ball-side, x3 never allows the simple pass out and stays in complete denial while the ball is in the post.

Reads in the Trap

Reads

Situation
The post kicks it out toward 1 or 2.
Read
That is the interceptor's pass.
Action
x1 picks it off or deflects it.
Situation
No steal or deflection is there and the outlet is caught.
Read
The interceptor is the closest help.
Action
x1 is first to close out on the catch.
Situation
The ball is in the post and 3 is ball-side.
Read
The simple pass out is the easy escape.
Action
x3 stays in complete denial.

Coach's questions

  1. Coach Kevin Keto · 01
    Why does x4 establish position behind before the trap arrives?
    What I'm listening forSo the post can't turn and score before x5 gets there, and the trap comes from the high side.
  2. Coach Kevin Keto · 02
    If you are x1 and nobody is open for you to steal, what do you do?
    What I'm listening forBe the first to close out to whoever receives the outlet pass.
